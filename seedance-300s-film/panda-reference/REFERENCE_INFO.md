# Panda Character Reference

## Purpose

This is the canonical visual reference for the single plush panda used throughout the 300-second Seedance 2.5 film.

## Reference Image

Place the generated panda image in this same folder with the filename:

`panda-reference.png`

If a different filename is used, update this file so every video prompt points to the exact image path.

## Character Identity

- One single plush panda toy.
- Approximately the same height as the one-year-old child.
- Noticeably fatter and wider than the child.
- Large, soft, round stuffed belly.
- Cute, funny, lovable and slightly clumsy.
- Black-and-white plush fabric with visible realistic fibers.
- Rounded ears.
- Expressive dark eyes.
- Short padded limbs.
- Consistent panda facial markings.
- Physical plush-toy appearance, not a real panda.
- Photorealistic cinematic presentation.

## Continuity Lock

Across all 10 videos, preserve exactly:

1. Head shape.
2. Ear shape and size.
3. Facial markings.
4. Eye placement and appearance.
5. Body height.
6. Body width.
7. Belly size and roundness.
8. Limb proportions.
9. Plush material and texture.
10. Black-and-white color pattern.

Do not redesign, resize, recolor, age, slim down, enlarge, or otherwise alter the panda.

## Scale Relationship

The panda should remain approximately the child's height while being substantially wider and heavier-looking because of its oversized stuffed body.

The panda must never become larger than the child by an implausible amount and must never shrink to a small toy.

## Required Use

Use this image as the **PANDA CHARACTER REFERENCE** in every Seedance 2.5 video prompt.

The panda is the same physical character from Video 01 through Video 10. It must never be replaced by another panda.

## Negative Constraints

- No second panda.
- No real panda.
- No duplicate panda.
- No cartoon transformation.
- No CGI-looking redesign.
- No clothing.
- No accessories.
- No different facial markings.
- No body-proportion changes.
- No random color changes.
- No text or logos.
- No watermark.
