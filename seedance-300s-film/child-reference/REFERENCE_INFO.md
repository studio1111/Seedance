# Child Character Reference

## Purpose

This is the canonical visual reference for the single one-year-old child used throughout the 300-second Seedance 2.5 film.

## Reference Image

Use the image filename:

`sakhi.Refernce.jpg`

The filename is the reference identifier. Do not require or imply any folder path for this image.

## Character Identity

- One single one-year-old child.
- The exact child shown in `sakhi.Refernce.jpg`.
- Preserve the child's identity consistently across all 10 videos.
- Natural, photorealistic human appearance.
- The child must remain clearly one year old throughout the entire film.

## Appearance Lock

Preserve exactly:

1. Face shape and facial identity.
2. Eyes, eye shape, placement and appearance.
3. Hair, hairstyle and hair characteristics.
4. Skin tone and natural skin appearance.
5. Body proportions.
6. Height and overall physical scale.
7. Apparent age and physical development.
8. Gray one-piece outfit.
9. Gray socks.
10. Overall natural appearance shown in the reference image.

Do not redesign, beautify, age, de-age, resize, recolor, restyle or otherwise reinterpret the child.

## Physical Development and Movement

The child is one year old.

Movement must remain physically believable for this age:

- Primarily crawls on hands and knees.
- Can move surprisingly quickly while crawling for comedic action.
- May make only brief, unstable attempts at walking.
- Cannot suddenly walk, run or move like an older child.
- No exaggerated athletic ability.
- No physically impossible movements.

## Scale Continuity

The child's height, body proportions and apparent age must remain unchanged across all 10 videos.

The child is the primary scale reference for the panda:

- The panda is approximately the child's height.
- The panda is substantially wider and fatter.
- The panda must never become implausibly large or small relative to the child.

## Required Use

Use `sakhi.Refernce.jpg` as the **CHILD CHARACTER REFERENCE** in every Seedance 2.5 video prompt.

The same child must appear from Video 01 through Video 10.

For each new video:

1. Keep the child reference active.
2. Use the previous video's final frame as the continuity/start-frame reference when supported.
3. Preserve the exact child identity from the reference image.
4. Do not allow the previous scene to alter the child's appearance.

## Continuity Lock

Across the complete film, preserve:

- Identity.
- Face.
- Hair.
- Skin tone.
- Age.
- Height.
- Body proportions.
- Gray clothing.
- Gray socks.
- Natural physical development.
- Consistent cinematic realism.

## Negative Constraints

- No second child.
- No duplicate child.
- No identity drift.
- No face redesign.
- No age change.
- No body-proportion changes.
- No height or scale drift.
- No clothing change.
- No color change to the gray outfit.
- No hairstyle change.
- No cartoon transformation.
- No CGI-looking human.
- No unrealistic walking or running.
- No text or logos.
- No watermark.
