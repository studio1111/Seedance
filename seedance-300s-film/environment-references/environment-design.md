# Environment Design — The Vanishing Panda

## Global environment
A lush cinematic bamboo forest with deep layered greens, shafts of sunlight, humid mist, moss, fern carpets, exposed roots, dark wet stones and a clear forest stream. Maintain coherent daylight and geography across all ten clips; locations should feel physically connected rather than unrelated.

## Route map
1. Helicopter approaches bamboo clearing; soft fern landing zone.
2. Dense bamboo maze, fallen log, muddy channel and stream fork.
3. Shallow stream, stepping stones, floating log, root arch and uphill trail.
4. Rope bridge across fern ravine; far bank leads to giant old tree.
5. Giant tree with glowing amber seed pods and rocky passage beyond.
6. Sloping rock corridor, rolling stones, fallen trunk and ridge bend.
7. Luminous grove with blue-green bulbs and roots, opening onto cliff path.
8. Narrow ridge, rock spire, puddle reflection and broad final shelf.
9. Final shelf above a wide river canyon and waterfall mist.
10. Same cliff shelf and river; distant eddies, panda unseen after the leap.

## Lighting and color
- Forest opening: warm sun shafts, emerald foliage and soft haze.
- Stream: bright highlights and cool water reflections.
- Rope bridge: open sunlight with readable depth and lush background.
- Seed-pod sequence: amber magical glow contrasted with green foliage; never imply real fire.
- Rocky corridor: earthy greens, slate rock and dust motes.
- Luminous grove: blue-green bulbs under canopy, daylight still visible in background.
- Finale: pale sky, river whitewater and waterfall mist; gray outfit remains legible.

## Physical response
Foliage bends in rotor wash; rope bridge oscillates with delayed wave motion; stream ripples and foam follow gravity; magical seed-pod bursts throw pollen, leaves and soft stylized fragments away from characters. No falling tree or debris may strike a character. No burning vegetation.
